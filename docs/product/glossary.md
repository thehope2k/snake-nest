# Glossary

**Nest**
The only structural unit in the platform. A group of people (friends, or
a workplace's informal side-channel) sharing one Chat and one Meet space.
No tenant/organization concept exists above it. A user can belong to
multiple Nests.

**Chat**
The persistent text-conversation view within a Nest. One of the two
product pillars.

**Meet**
The ad hoc voice/video session view within a Nest. One of the two product
pillars.

**Doghouse**
A Meet sub-feature: forcibly muting another participant's outbound audio
(server-side) for a fixed duration, visibly and consensually, as a bit —
signals "we're talking about you" without doing it secretly. See
[../features/meet.md](../features/meet.md).

**Roast Assistant** *(working name, idea stage)*
A Chat sub-feature concept: AI-assisted rewriting of a message to be more
playful/hurtful/funny before sending. Details deferred — see
[../features/chat.md](../features/chat.md).

**Confession Booth** *(idea stage)*
A Chat sub-feature: anonymous (peer-facing, not moderator-facing)
posting mode within a Nest.

**Roast Leaderboard / W-L Scoreboard** *(idea stage)*
Chat sub-features: lightweight, casual tallying of Doghouse counts or
self/peer-declared wins-losses, per Nest.

**Soundboard** *(idea stage)*
A Meet sub-feature: short audio stings any participant can trigger for
the whole room.
